# Uthao full audit, 2026-11-08

Scope: the whole game at commit `fb560c1` (`main`, same as `afnan/T9.15-asphalt-orientation`), checked against `docs/MASTER.md` (wins over `docs/GDD.md`) and `docs/PLAYTEST.md`.

Method: twelve explorers, one per dimension (boot and menus, driving physics, rendering, traffic and roads, ride loop and economy, phone/HUD/map, campaign and endings, save lifecycle, input and accessibility, content and i18n, audio, performance and mobile). Each ran its own dev server and drove the game in headless Chromium, and read the code. 83 candidates were raised. Each was checked by three verifiers with different lenses: code trace, live reproduction, and spec and severity. A candidate is confirmed when at least two verifiers say it is real. 76 were confirmed and 7 were rejected (listed at the end).

Caveats: the verifiers ran on Haiku, so severities are a first ranking. Runtime checks used headless Chromium on desktop viewports unless noted, not a real phone or a real PC. The audit did not test audio output, so the audio results come from code only. Nothing here was signed off by a human playtest, and GATE-M1 is still open.

## Summary

- 0 blockers, 17 high, 25 medium, 34 low. Most of the high items are about the player being unable to get back into the game, or the game being silent or unclear.
- The most visible problems for a new player: the title screen never offers Continue; exiting mid-tutorial leaves the tutorial card on top of the title; the game has no sound at all; raw translation keys appear on pause and save screens; touch players lose pause, save, map and phone controls.
- Several items from the 2026-11-06 audit still hold at this commit (marked "still present" below). Explorers reported others as fixed in their logs (campaign-endings, save-lifecycle, content-i18n, rendering-world). I did not re-verify each prior item.

## High

**Boot, menus and flow**
- **H-1. Continue never shows.** A saved career cannot be resumed from the title. `src/ui/App.tsx:356-372` renders `MainMenu` without `hasSave`, so it defaults to false (`src/ui/menus/MainMenu.tsx:15`, Continue gated at `:136`). `SaveSystem.hasAnySave` (`src/sim/save/SaveSystem.ts:322`) has no caller. Reproduced after a reload. The only other route is Pause > Load, which exists only in-game.
- **H-2. Exit mid-tutorial blocks the title.** `onExitToMainMenu` (`src/ui/App.tsx:394-397`) never clears `tutorialSignal`, and `FirstRideTutorial` renders on any screen (`App.tsx:238`). Its full-screen layer (z-index 9995) covers the title. Reproduced: Settings cannot be clicked until a reload.

**Driving and physics**
- **H-3. Hard steering bleeds speed.** Full lock at 40 km/h drops to about 19 km/h in one second (`src/physics/bikeModel.ts:133`).
- **H-4. Collisions give no feedback.** Wobble and crash events have no visual, HUD or audio response (still present, `src/physics/MotorcycleController.ts:135`).
- **H-5. Crash leaves the bike wedged.** After a crash the bike stays against the kerb with no reset (`src/sim/GameSession.ts:372`).

**Rendering**
- **H-6. No ground plane in district or world mode.** Buildings and road edges float over a sky-blue void (`src/render/Renderer.ts`).
- **H-7. Camera anti-clip uses stale boxes.** It checks 7 test-scene boxes, so real building colliders never reach it (still present, `src/render/Renderer.ts`).

**Traffic and soft-locks**
- **H-8. Soft-lock after static contact.** The bike cannot move, reverse or recover (`src/physics/MotorcycleController.ts`).

**Campaign and economy**
- **H-9. Buying the bike ending spends the price first.** The ending is then judged as "A Hard Lesson" (still present, `src/sim/GameManager.ts`).

**Save**
- **H-10. Continue missing after reload.** Same root cause as H-1 (`src/ui/App.tsx`).
- **H-11. New Career overwrites the existing save with no confirmation** (`src/ui/bindings.ts`).
- **H-12. Loading a day-end autosave in a new session charges living expenses twice** (`src/sim/save/SaveSystem.ts`).
- **H-13. Save failure gives no feedback.** The save panel closes and the failure alert never shows (`src/ui/App.tsx`).

**Content and i18n**
- **H-14. Pause menu shows raw keys** `pause.save` and `pause.load` (`src/ui/menus/Pause.tsx`).

**Audio**
- **H-15. The game is silent.** `AudioManager` is never constructed, so no sound plays (still present, `src/audio/AudioManager.ts`).

**Input and mobile**
- **H-16. Touch throttle and brake stay latched** after the finger lifts, while steer is held (`src/input/InputManager.ts`).
- **H-17. Touch players have no pause, save, map, phone, horn or camera control** (`src/ui/hud/Hud.tsx`).

H-1 and H-10 share one root cause (no Continue button), so fixing H-1 fixes both.

## Medium

**Boot and settings**
- New Career has no confirmation and replaces the career (`src/ui/bindings.ts:138-148`, `src/main.ts:280-283`).
- The quality setting is saved but never applied to the renderer (still present, `src/main.ts:128-131`).

**Physics and collisions**
- Traffic contact re-fires every tick while overlapping, so one hit can count as many crashes (`src/sim/GameSession.ts:386`).
- Wobble and crash flags never reach the player (`src/physics/bikeModel.ts:133-138`).

**Rendering**
- Night is near black at 22:30 and 03:00 (still present, `src/render/WeatherFx.ts`).
- Road markings, zebra crossings and puddles are binned to the origin chunk and vanish away from it (still present, `src/render/roadMesh.ts`).

**Traffic and pedestrians**
- Traffic lights are simulated but never read, so vehicles ignore signals (`src/sim/traffic/signals.ts`).
- Traffic never yields to or sees the bike (`src/sim/traffic/TrafficManager.ts`).
- Pedestrians ignore vehicles, accidents and the bike except for the horn (`src/sim/traffic/pedestrians.ts`).

**Ride loop and economy**
- Mission and encounter money is booked as trip income with no trip, and the HUD shows it as earnings (still present, `src/sim/mission/MissionManager.ts:248`, `src/sim/events/effects.ts:28`).
- The friend-parcel encounter pays 120 at once and is called a trip, but no delivery happens (`data/encounters/passenger.json:39-60`).
- Tips and bonuses are hard-coded to zero in the live path (still present, `src/sim/ride/RideManager.ts:581-582`).

**Phone, HUD and map**
- The HUD says "no passenger" while a fare is active (`src/sim/ride/rideView.ts`).
- The drop-off objective shows a raw road node id, not a place name (`src/sim/ride/rideView.ts`).

**Campaign**
- The prototype notice says the purchase does not affect the ending, which contradicts the code (`data/dialogue/en/endings.json`).

**Save and i18n**
- Raw keys appear in save and load slot dialogs, and English mode still shows Bangla-only labels there (`src/ui/menus/SaveSlots.tsx`).
- Journal title shows the raw key `journal_title`, and the close button shows `btn_close` (`src/ui/journal/Journal.tsx`).
- The pause subtitle is hard-coded English (`src/ui/menus/Pause.tsx`).

**Input and accessibility**
- Keyboard focus leaks behind open Settings: Tab reaches and Enter opens hidden menu buttons (`src/ui/menus/Settings.tsx`).
- Reduce motion changes CSS only. The 3D camera FOV kick is not gated, and the label promises a shake that does not exist (still present, `src/ui/menus/Settings.tsx`).
- The colour-blind setting changes one minimap route colour and nothing else. `colorblindPalette` is unused (still present, `src/ui/settingsEffects.ts`).

**Audio**
- Volume sliders save and reload but control nothing, and the UI does not say so (still present, `src/ui/menus/Settings.tsx`).

**Mobile**
- Touch throttle, brake and steer zones are invisible (`src/input/layout.ts`).

## Low

Boot and settings: volume sliders and reduce-motion are stored but nothing reads them (still present). Credits ignore Escape. The English tagline sits under the Bangla title (`src/ui/menus/MainMenu.tsx:117-121`).

Driving: grinding a static obstacle fires `bike/collision` every frame (849 events in 45 s). Releasing the throttle does not slow the bike, and speed never decays. Wet braking is about 43% longer than dry, slightly above the spec band (`src/config/balance.ts:151`).

Rendering: heavy rain renders as large floating squares, not drops (`src/render/WeatherFx.ts`). World mode's HUD objective shows a raw node id.

Ride and economy: a request can arrive while a story card covers the screen, and the phone cannot open over the card (`src/ui/App.tsx:92-96`). Disputed trips settle the full fare, so the dispute only changes the label (still present, `src/sim/ride/RideManager.ts:576-596`). The negotiation panel mixes Bangla and Western numerals (`src/ui/negotiation/NegotiationPanel.tsx`).

Phone and map: the tutorial title shows a developer doc reference. The app name is spelled two ways. The Bangla HUD and passenger panel hard-code English labels (still present, `src/ui/hud/Hud.tsx`). The player marker on the map is hidden under the location chip row at the default view (`src/ui/map/MapScreen.tsx`).

Campaign: the vendor-discount encounter gives the Chapter 2 flag only on the standard choice, so a grant choice can stall `m2_profit_lesson` (`data/encounters/payment.json`). The survive objective counts refusing as well as surviving.

Save: sleep does not autosave and does not check that the shift ended (`src/sim/GameManager.ts`). Ratings history is saved but dropped on load (still present).

Input and accessibility: text scaling covers only the subtitle; the rest of the UI has fixed sizes. The first-ride tutorial banner counts as a modal, so Tab does not open the phone. Hotkeys are hard-coded and ignore the bindings config (still present, `src/ui/App.tsx`). Gamepad A with no focus only focuses the first item. A quick tap of C is dropped; holding C works (`src/input/InputManager.ts`). Overlays have no keyboard focus target on open.

Content and i18n: hard-coded English text appears in Bangla mode, and a dev reference leaks into the tutorial (still present).

Audio: no mute control. Volume defaults disagree with the audio defaults and with each other (`src/ui/store.ts`). The ambient slider has no music channel to feed. `updateAmbientGains` compounds on every master change (`src/audio/AudioManager.ts`). Audio unit tests only check that calls do not throw (still present). `bikes.json` `audioPitch` is parsed and never used (still present).

Performance and mobile: speed never decays after the throttle is released. The Credits button is clipped and unreachable by touch in landscape 844x390 (`src/ui/menus/MainMenu.tsx`).

## Rejected by verifiers

- English title reads "UTHÃO": deliberate in `data/dialogue/en/ui.json:169`, asserted by a test.
- Requests use a 15-second timer the player cannot read: the timer never counts down, so the mechanism is wrong.
- Phone does not open with Tab while a request card shows: the card is the tutorial modal, not the phone's request card.
- Prior audit M6 and M19 not confirmed: M6 was fixed in T9.14, and M19 is refuted because the map has a keyboard-reachable POI list.
- Map waypoint placement is pointer-only: refuted by the `map-poi-list` buttons.
- English title spelled UTHÃO: same as the first rejected item.
- Audio routing mismatch: not reachable, because `AudioManager` is imported nowhere in the app. This is H-15 in another form.
