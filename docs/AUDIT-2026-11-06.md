# Uthao full audit, 2026-11-06

Scope: the whole repo at `afnan/T8.7-gate-m1-prototype` (merged to `main` as `cd5ca37`), checked against `docs/MASTER.md`, which wins over `docs/GDD.md`.

Method: seven read-only auditors, one per dimension (spec conformance, UI wiring, simulation and economy, physics/render/perf, content data, save and input, test quality). Every candidate was then checked by two adversarial verifiers who tried to refute it. 86 candidates were raised, 69 were confirmed, and 17 were refuted or could not be verified (not listed). Several confirmed items were reported by more than one dimension, so they are merged below.

Caveats: this is static analysis plus code tracing. Nothing here was played through by hand except the probes already run this session (new career and tutorial, the free-ride endless mode, and the encounter-card freeze). Some verifiers ran on a smaller model, so treat the severities as a first ranking, not a final judgement. Spot-checks on three high-impact items (#13, #18, #20 below) held up.

## Where the suite stands

- Unit suite: 476/476 pass. `tsc`, ESLint on `src` and `tests`, and data validation pass.
- Playwright: 41 pass, 1 skipped (pre-existing).

Those results do not catch most of what follows. Many of the findings sit in places the tests never touch: gamepad, focus, the campaign past Chapter 2, and the accessibility settings.

## Blockers (the game cannot be played as specified)

**B1. Campaign stops at Chapter 2.** Five flag objectives can never complete because nothing sets their flags: `profit_lesson_done` (`data/missions/ch2.json:61`), `pressuredSpeeding` (`ch3.json:61`), `helpedHospitalFree` (`ch3.json:106`), `cooperatedPolice` (`ch4.json:100`), and `ch6_decision_made` (`ch6.json:83`). Mission unlocks are prerequisite chains, so `m2_profit_lesson` blocks everything after it. `tests/unit/sim/campaignChapters.test.ts:114` calls `completeMission` directly, which hides the gap.

**B2. Campaign stops at Chapter 4.** `m4_wrong_pickup` references encounter ids that do not exist: `crime_enc_wrong_pickup` (`ch4.json:54`) and `police_checkpoint` (`ch4.json:60`). The real ids are `enc_wrong_pickup_pin` and `enc_traffic_checkpoint`. Also, `MissionManager.ts:135` passes the instance id to objectives, but instance ids have the form `inst_<defId>_…`, so even correct definition ids would never match.

**B3. Gamepad cannot operate the game's UI.** Gamepad buttons are only read by `InputManager`. Nothing routes them to menus, the phone, the map, pause, or the choice cards. A gamepad-only player cannot start a career, cannot answer an encounter, and cannot pause (`src/main.ts:303`, `src/input/InputManager.ts:147`).

**B4. Keyboard focus cannot leave the first element.** `src/ui/App.tsx:76-80` calls `preventDefault` on Tab and uses it to toggle the phone. Tab never moves focus, so buttons in menus and modals are unreachable without a mouse.

## High

**H1. Fabricated ending and placeholder endings.** `MissionManager.ts:70-74` fires `ending_v1_first_week` on every `day/ended` from day 6. The screen shows ৳0 savings and made-up 25% identity tendencies (`src/ui/store.ts:508`), and it reappears at the end of every later shift. The five MASTER §3 endings are never evaluated in live play, and the career decision at Chapter 6 cannot be made (`store.ts:252`, `Ending.tsx:261-271`).

**H2. Random passengers show raw keys.** `data/archetypes.json:1` names are undefined keys such as `p_abdul_kader`. The `|| passengerId` fallback never triggers because the key string is truthy. This shows in negotiation on the core loop in both languages.

**H3. Near-miss fires every frame at speed.** In a physics session, the bike's own capsule is hit by its ray (`src/physics/MotorcycleController.ts:281`). About 60 `bike/nearMiss` events per second are recorded, and the trip rating reaches its 1.0 floor regardless of driving.

**H4. Only rickshaws are drawn.** Cars, buses, motorcycles, and CNG autos collide with the bike but are not rendered (`src/render/people.ts:385`). Crashes and bumps appear to have no cause.

**H5. Hidden tab overwrites the career from the main menu.** The visibility handler (`src/main.ts:197`) calls `SaveSystem.bindAutosave`, which saves the current state to the auto slot (`src/sim/save/SaveSystem.ts:366-371`). If the player is on the main menu with a blank game, the blank state replaces the real career.

**H6. Storage failure reports success.** When `localStorage` hits quota or throws, saves fall back to memory and still report success (`src/sim/save/SaveSystem.ts:231`). The player reloads and finds the latest save missing.

**H7. Trips after the 23:00 shift end go missing.** A trip that finishes after the forced shift end is posted to the ledger but left out of the day summary, and sleep zeroes the day counters (`src/sim/GameManager.ts:331`).

**H8. Advance money creates unlimited debt.** `EconomyManager.askAdvance` (`src/sim/economy/EconomyManager.ts:369`) adds the amount to both cash and debt with no cap, and it emits a bus event but never writes the ledger. Cash and ledger therefore stop reconciling. Verified against source.

**H9. Delay rent is a dead control that reports success.** The command returns success, the landlady relationship drops, and the full rent is still charged at shift end (`src/sim/GameManager.ts:624`).

**H10. 18 of 21 encounter definitions can never fire in live play.** `GameManager.tick` evaluates only the `roaming` phase (`src/sim/GameManager.ts:359`). The on-trip, pickup and drop-off encounters, including the MASTER §19 suspicious-pickup and dark-alley events, are never evaluated.

**H11. Robbery has no consequences.** `data/encounters/crime.json:59`: "try to flee" closes the encounter with no effect, and "comply" costs nothing. MASTER §11 requires lost cash, damage, or injury.

**H12. Accidents are absent.** A crash stops the bike and drops the rating, with no response choices, no compensation, and no injury (`src/sim/GameSession.ts:363-381`). MASTER §13 and the §19 basic accident system require them.

**H13. New career keeps most of the old career's state.** `GameSession.newCareer` (`src/sim/GameSession.ts:503`) keeps the old debt, savings, owned bikes, upgrades, relationships, and chapter.

**H14. Accessibility controls are stored but do nothing.** Reduce motion and colorblind route have no effect (`src/ui/menus/Settings.tsx:287`), and colorblind mode does not exist. The rules make both mandatory. The tests only check that the settings persist (`tests/unit/ui/accessibility.test.ts:21`).

**H15. Volume sliders have no effect.** The saved values are never applied to audio (`src/ui/menus/Settings.tsx:345`).

**H16. Language does not persist correctly.** A saved English setting is back to Bangla after reload, and Cancel does not revert the language for the session (`Settings.tsx:46`).

**H17. Camera key and gamepad Select are dead.** `C` and gamepad Select are bound (`src/input/bindings.ts:12`), but the camera does not change.

**H18. No regression test for the stale-negotiation guard.** A negotiation action that arrives after the ride moves on is applied to the new ride (`src/ui/bindings.ts:59`), and no test fails if the guard is removed.

## Medium

- **M1.** The data validator misses several reference classes, so bad mission and encounter references pass `npm run check` (`scripts/validate-data.mjs:40`).
- **M2.** English mode shows Bangla shop signs. `data/dialogue/en/signs.json` is never read (`src/render/signage.ts:34`).
- **M3.** The chase camera's collider list is a one-time snapshot, so streamed-in buildings are not avoided. The camera clips through walls in new districts (`src/render/Renderer.ts:253`).
- **M4.** Lane markings, puddles, and zebra crossings are binned to the origin chunk and hidden outside 300 m of it (`src/render/roadMesh.ts:195`).
- **M5.** District streaming overruns the 4 ms frame budget, and per-collider physics stepping makes it worse (`src/physics/PhysicsWorld.ts:48`).
- **M6.** The minimap rotates the wrong way against the bike and is offset by 90 degrees (`src/render/minimap.ts:91`).
- **M7.** Asphalt is oriented wrong on diagonal segments (`src/render/roadMesh.ts:124`).
- **M8.** Rain and camera timing depend on frame rate (`src/render/Renderer.ts:311`).
- **M9.** Held keys stick after the window loses focus (`src/input/InputManager.ts:228`).
- **M10.** Escape handling uses a stale `showSettings`. Escape can resume the game under an open Settings modal, or open the pause screen behind Settings (`src/ui/App.tsx:100`). Two reports merged.
- **M11.** `negotiationHistory` is in the snapshot but not the save, so it resets on load (`src/sim/save/SaveSystem.ts:82`).
- **M12.** Loading a save mid-fare keeps the live trip, so it settles into the restored economy (`src/ui/bindings.ts:157`).
- **M13.** Tips and bonuses are hard-coded to zero in live play (`src/sim/ride/RideManager.ts:581`).
- **M14.** The karma panel omits most consequence systems and is not labelled as partial (`src/ui/journal/Journal.tsx:292`).
- **M15.** Police handling is minimal, and no police encounter fires in play (`data/encounters/route.json:174`).
- **M16.** No test covers the gamepad input path or any keyboard-or-gamepad e2e flow (`src/input/InputManager.ts:125`).
- **M17.** The known Disputed-status defect has no test (`docs/QUESTIONS.md:41`).
- **M18.** The renderer e2e spec asserts nothing about FPS, and its screenshot check passes on a black frame (`tests/e2e/renderer.spec.ts:22`).
- **M19.** Waypoint selection on the map works only with a pointer (`src/ui/map/MapScreen.tsx:496`).
- **M20.** Some visible text is hard-coded in English or bilingual, bypassing i18n: the tutorial, garage stats, install button, and HUD passenger label (`src/ui/App.tsx:436`).

## Low

- **L1.** `bikes.json` `tankCapacityL` and `audioPitch` are never read (`data/bikes.json:14`).
- **L2.** Renderer and CityBuilder have no teardown, so a context restore leaks physics colliders and GPU objects (`src/render/Renderer.ts:256`).
- **L3.** Some render and sim features are unwired: streetlights, flood water, adaptive quality, and asset factories (`src/render/WeatherFx.ts:30`).
- **L4.** Minimap text is hard-coded English (`src/render/minimap.ts:225`).
- **L5.** The debug API is enabled in production builds behind `?debug=1` (`src/debug/debugApi.ts:87`). The `teleport` helper only places a waypoint.
- **L6.** Rating history is saved but dropped on load (`src/sim/save/SaveSystem.ts:109`).
- **L7.** Input binding persistence is dead, and a malformed binding silently disables its action with no fallback (`src/input/InputManager.ts:70`).
- **L8.** `payExpense` accepts a negative amount (`src/sim/GameManager.ts:644`).
- **L9.** The `time` encounter effect skips the clock side effects that other advances run (`src/sim/events/effects.ts:43`).
- **L10.** The phone has no chat and no earnings view, and the gap is not disclosed (`src/ui/phone/Phone.tsx:179`).
- **L11.** Disputed trips settle the full fare, so the dispute changes only the label (`src/sim/ride/RideManager.ts:576`).
- **L12.** `fastForwardTrip` is an unimplemented no-op with no caller (`src/sim/GameSession.ts:557`).
- **L13.** A bike test lower bound would pass a double charge (`tests/unit/sim/bikes.test.ts:138`).
- **L14.** Audio and minimap tests assert only that calls do not throw (`tests/unit/audio/audio.test.ts:33`).
- **L15.** Document-presence tests inflate the pass count (`tests/unit/qa/testMatrix.test.ts:48`).
- **L16.** Hotkeys are hard-coded, bindings are ignored, and the numpad choice keys are silent with NumLock off (`src/ui/App.tsx:83`).
- **L17.** Escape does not close the garage or journal opened in-game (`src/ui/App.tsx:108`).

## Suggested order

1. **Input and focus (B3, B4, H17, M16, M19, L16).** This blocks every human playtest, including Gate M1.
2. **The fabricated ending (H1).** It is visible in ordinary play from day 6.
3. **Save integrity (H5, H6, H13, M11, M12).**
4. **Money (H7, H8, H9).**
5. **Campaign progression (B1, B2, H2, and the Chapter 6 decision in H1).** This is mostly content and wiring, with a test that walks each mission chain to the end.
6. **Encounters and consequences (H10, H11, H12, M13, M15).**
7. **Settings and accessibility (H14, H15, H16).** These are mandatory under the UI rules.
8. **Physics and rendering (H3, H4, M3 to M9).**

Fixes should carry regression tests that fail before the fix, per the test standard.
