## 3. Bangladesh-Specific Procedural Models
To preserve authentic Dhaka street ambience and guarantee zero-dependency crash immunity, the following models remain procedural in `src/render/vehicles.ts` and `src/render/props.ts` (and act as fallbacks when offline or in headless Node):

- **Motorcycle (`MotorcycleMesh`)**: Player's bike, pillion passenger attachment, fork steering and lean kinematics.
- **CNG Auto-Rickshaw (`createCNGMesh`)**: Dhaka green 3-wheeler with yellow skirt, front slope, wireframe iron safety cages.
- **Cycle Rickshaw (`createRickshawMesh`)**: Manual 3-wheeler tricycle with rear carriage, bright canvas hood canopy, and folk rickshaw art backplate.
- **Local Bus (`createBusMesh`)**: 8m commuter bus with colourful Dhaka route stripes, roof luggage rack, and destination banner board.
- **Tea Stall / Tong Dokan (`createTeaStallMesh`)**: Corrugated iron roof, bamboo posts, wooden counter, aluminum kettle, and roadside bench.
- **Fuel Station Canopy (`createFuelCanopyMesh`)**: Green overhead canopy with support pillars and dual pump dispensers.
- **Mechanic Garage Shutter (`createGarageShutterMesh`)**: Brick workshop frame, corrugated blue-grey roll-up shutter, and Kamal bhai signboard.
- **Footbridge (`createFootbridgeMesh`)**: Overhead pedestrian overpass spanning road traffic with safety mesh railings and stair towers.
- **Rooftop Water Tank (`createRooftopWaterTankMesh`)**: Black cylindrical Gazi-style water tank on concrete roof pilings.
- **Overhead Wires (`createOverheadWiresMesh`)**: Tangled black cable bundles draped across utility poles.
- **Shop Signboards (`createShopSignboardMesh`)**: Street storefront display banners in Dhaka commercial aesthetics.

## 4. Pipeline Automation & Runtime Loader
- **Manifest**: `assets.manifest.json` defines verified packs (with SHA-256 hashes), curated `models` (`AssetCatalogEntry`), `audio`, and `proceduralModels`.
- **Fetch**: `node scripts/fetch-assets.mjs` downloads official CC0 Kenney ZIP archives into gitignored `assets-src/` (using SHA-256 caching) and verifies `License.txt` in each archive.
- **Optimize**: `node scripts/optimize-assets.mjs` transforms source `.glb` files via `@gltf-transform/core`, `@gltf-transform/functions`, `@gltf-transform/extensions`, `meshoptimizer`, and `sharp` (`dedup`, `prune`, `flatten`, `quantize`, `meshopt` `EXT_meshopt_compression`, WebP textures resized `<= 512px`), and enforces the raw & gzipped size budgets.
- **Run full pipeline**: `npm run assets`
- **Runtime Loader (`src/render/assets.ts`)**: `AssetManager` wires Three.js `GLTFLoader` + `MeshoptDecoder`, exposes `init()`, `preloadCore(onProgress)`, `loadModel(id, lod)`, `getInstancedPrototype(id)`, and `dispose()`, normalizes ground pivots (`minY = 0`) with catalog scaling, deinterleaves/dequantizes prototype geometries to `Float32` for `InstancedMesh`, and gracefully falls back to procedural geometry in headless Node or offline failures.
